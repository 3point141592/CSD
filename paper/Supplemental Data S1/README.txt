Supplemental Data S1

Seed-level results, study metadata and summary statistics accompanying the CSD manuscript.

Contents
- seed_results.csv: seed-level result index, metrics and links to source records.
- study_register.csv: study purposes, models, dimensions, seeds and archive locations.
- ablation_means_and_sd.csv: mean coverage and sample standard deviations for the prepared-feature and feature-width comparisons.
- recalculated_coverage_means.csv: recalculated coverage summaries, seed counts and completeness indicators.
- paper_to_files.csv: mapping from manuscript tables, figures and claims to their source records and analysis commands.
- data_dictionary.csv: field definitions, units and missing-value markers.

Fractions range from 0 to 1. Sample standard deviations describe variation across seeds. Missing-value and not-applicable markers are defined in data_dictionary.csv; they are not numerical zeros.

Source paths in the CSVs are relative to the full research archive. The referenced code, model checkpoints and detailed records are supplied in that archive. Paths beginning ../01_Journal_Files refer to the accompanying journal files, also provided in the repository's paper/ folder.
